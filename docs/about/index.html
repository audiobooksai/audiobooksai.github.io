About AudiobooksAI¶

Welcome to AudiobooksAI, your home for free, high-quality audiobooks of public domain classics! Our mission is to make timeless literature accessible to all by releasing one professionally narrated audiobook every week.

Our Story¶

We’re passionate about bringing stories like Heidi, Pride and Prejudice, and Moby Dick to life through audio. Using advanced text-to-speech (TTS) technology, we create clear, engaging narrations of public domain works—books free from copyright that belong to everyone. Our platform is simple and open to all, delivering a new audiobook every Monday, no subscriptions or fees required.

What We Do¶

  • Weekly Releases: A new TTS-narrated audiobook every Monday, free to stream or download.
  • Public Domain Focus: Timeless stories from adventure to romance, accessible to all.
  • Easy Access: A user-friendly website with features like searchable catalogs coming soon.
  • Community-Driven: We welcome your book suggestions and feedback to shape AudiobooksAI.

How It Works¶

Our TTS technology produces natural-sounding narrations, and our cloud-based system ensures fast, buffer-free audio delivery. It’s like a digital library in your pocket, ready to share classic stories anytime.

Why Public Domain?¶

Public domain books are cultural treasures, free for everyone to enjoy. Our weekly releases bring these classics to new listeners, making literature open and inclusive.

Join Us¶

AudiobooksAI is a community for story lovers. Visit our site to listen to our latest release, suggest a book, or connect with us on social media. Let’s keep these stories alive, one week at a time!
