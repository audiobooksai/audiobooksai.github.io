About The Picture of Dorian Gray¶

The Picture of Dorian Gray centers on Dorian, a handsome young man whose portrait is painted by artist Basil Hallward. Influenced by the hedonistic Lord Henry Wotton, Dorian wishes his portrait would age instead of him, a wish that mysteriously comes true. As Dorian indulges in a life of pleasure and sin, his youthful appearance remains intact while the portrait bears the marks of his corrupted soul. This gothic tale weaves themes of vanity, decadence, and the pursuit of eternal youth, culminating in a haunting exploration of inner decay versus outward perfection.

Set against the opulent backdrop of Victorian England, the story is rich with Wilde’s sharp wit and philosophical musings, making it a compelling listen for those intrigued by psychology, art, and morality.
