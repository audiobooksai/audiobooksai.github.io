About Heidi¶

Heidi follows the journey of a young orphan girl who finds joy and purpose in the mountains of Switzerland. Sent to live with her gruff grandfather in the Alps, Heidi’s cheerful spirit transforms those around her, from her grandfather to her friend Peter, the goatherd. This story of resilience, nature, and human connection is perfect for listeners of all ages, whether you’re revisiting a childhood favorite or discovering it for the first time.

With our TTS narration, you’ll hear Heidi’s adventures come alive with clear, engaging voices that capture the warmth and charm of Johanna Spyri’s classic. Whether you’re hiking, relaxing, or commuting, let Heidi’s story transport you to the breathtaking Alps!
