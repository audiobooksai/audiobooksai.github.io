About Pride and Prejudice¶

Pride and Prejudice follows Elizabeth Bennet, a sharp-witted and independent young woman from a modest English family, as she navigates love and societal expectations in Regency-era England. The story centers on her evolving relationship with the wealthy and reserved Mr. Fitzwilliam Darcy. Initially repelled by his pride and her own prejudice, Elizabeth’s encounters with Darcy—spanning balls, misunderstandings, and family drama—gradually reveal his true character. Meanwhile, her sisters’ romantic entanglements, including the impulsive Lydia’s elopement with the roguish Mr. Wickham, add layers of humor and tension.

Set against the picturesque countryside estates and lively social scenes of early 19th-century Britain, the novel explores themes of class, marriage, and self-discovery with Austen’s signature irony and insight. From the iconic first line, “It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife,” to the satisfying resolution of Elizabeth and Darcy’s love, it’s a story that resonates across generations. Our TTS narration brings the lively dialogue and emotional depth to life with clear, expressive voices, perfect for a cozy listen at home or during a leisurely walk.
